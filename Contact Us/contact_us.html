Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers regarding clubs, website updates, and student contributions.

How do I report an issue or suggest a feature on Cluvora?

You can message the 3 website builders directly using the form above or email any of the developers listed in the team section.

Can I update my club or society's information?

Yes! Club coordinators can reach out to CoSA or mention updates in the contact form with your official club coordinator email.

Who can join student clubs at IIT Bhilai?

All enrolled students (B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc, and Ph.D.) are eligible to participate and register in all tech, cultural, and sports clubs.

How can I contribute to the Cluvora codebase?

Cluvora is an open initiative built by students. Contact the builders via their GitHub profiles above to get involved in ongoing enhancements.
